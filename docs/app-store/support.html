ClipDock Support / 技术支持

Quick Start / 快速开始

  1. Open ClipDock and allow Photos access. It scans on its own and shows Scanning... until videos are listed.
  2. On the USB Drive card, tap Change › and choose the destination folder.
  3. Tap Choose myself, or tap Pick the 20 largest videos.
  4. Tap a video to select or clear it.
  5. Tap Copy to Drive and stay in ClipDock. The screen says Keep ClipDock in the foreground; locking or switching apps pauses the copy.
  6. Deleting originals from Photos is optional and is available only after their external copies pass size verification. Don't Delete Yet leaves them in Photos.
  1. 打开 ClipDock 并允许相册访问。它会自行扫描，还没有列出视频时显示 扫描中...。
  2. 在 U 盘 卡片上点 更换 ›，选择目标文件夹。
  3. 点 自己挑，或点 挑最大的 20 个视频。
  4. 点一下视频即可选中或取消。
  5. 点 拷到 U 盘，并留在 ClipDock。画面会显示 请保持 ClipDock 在前台，锁屏或切走会暂停。
  6. 是否删除相册原片由你决定；只有外置副本通过大小校验后，才可删除对应原片。点 先不删 会把原片留在相册里。

If Photos access is already allowed, the scan starts without asking again. Copy to Drive stays unavailable until a video is selected and the folder is writable. Until the size check finishes, delete reads Delete originals (available after verification). It then asks Delete Originals? with Delete and Cancel.

若相册权限已经允许，扫描会直接开始，不再询问。至少选中 1 个视频且文件夹可写之前，拷到 U 盘 不可用。大小校验完成前，删除按钮是 删除原片（校验完成后可用）。之后会询问 删除原视频？，按钮为 删除 和 取消。

Troubleshooting / 常见问题

A) Migration fails with permission error / 迁移提示无权限

  • Reconnect your external drive.
  • Tap the saved USB Drive card to open External Storage, then tap Recheck Folder Access.
  • If it still fails, tap Change › and choose the destination folder again.
  • 重新插拔外接存储。
  • 点已保存的 U 盘 卡片，打开 外接存储，再点 重新检查目录权限。
  • 若仍失败，点 更换 ›，重新选择目标文件夹。

B) Video size shows "--" or ≈ / 视频大小显示 "--" 或 ≈

A size that is not known yet is shown as --. A video that is only in iCloud can show an estimate prefixed with ≈. Pick Videos also shows ☁︎ Sizes for videos that are only in iCloud are estimates.

还不知道的大小显示为 --。只在 iCloud 的视频可以显示以 ≈ 开头的估算大小。挑视频 页面还会显示 ☁︎ 只在 iCloud 的视频大小是估算值。
